Import Checking Account CSV

Every week, export/download your checking account history CSV from your bank and upload it here. Already-imported transactions are automatically skipped — only new ones are added, so it's safe to re-upload an overlapping export.

Auto-Reconcile Bills

Runs automatically after every import: matches new transactions to your tracked bills (by merchant, amount, and date) and marks them paid automatically, so you don't have to click Mark Paid yourself. Each match below can be undone with one click if something matched wrong.

📱 ntfy (push notification to your phone) - easiest option

ntfy is a free pub-sub notification service: anyone (or anything, like this app) can publish a message to a "topic", and anyone subscribed to that same topic on their phone instantly gets it as a push notification. There's no account and no sign-up. The topic name works like a password, so the order below matters - if you test before subscribing, the message sends successfully but nobody's listening, so nothing shows up on your phone and it looks broken when it isn't.

  1. Install the free ntfy app: ntfy.sh for iOS/Android (or web at ntfy.sh).
  2. Click "Generate a topic name" below (or make up your own private, hard-to-guess one).
  3. In the ntfy app, tap + Subscribe to topic and enter that exact same topic name.
  4. Come back here, check Enabled, and click Save.
  5. Then click Send Test - it should show up on your phone within a few seconds.

💬 SMS via Carrier Email Gateway (free)

Every carrier secretly runs an email-to-text bridge: an email sent to yournumber@carrier-domain arrives on your phone as a text. It's free but not officially supported, so delivery can be delayed or occasionally filtered as spam.

  1. Fill in the SMTP card below first (this is what actually sends the email) - a Gmail address with an app password works well; your normal Gmail password won't work here.
  2. Enter your phone number and pick your carrier below.
  3. Check Enabled, click Save, then Send Test.

📨 SMTP (used for gateway SMS and/or email digest)

One-time setup shared by the two email-based options above/below. If using Gmail: go to your Google App Passwords page, create one named "Budget App", and paste the 16-character password it gives you here (not your normal Gmail password - Google blocks that for apps like this).

📧 Email Digest

Sends the same digest to a regular email address. Uses the SMTP settings above.

☎️ Twilio SMS (paid, most reliable)

Skip this unless the free options above are too unreliable for you. Requires a Twilio account (~$1/mo for a phone number plus a fraction of a cent per text). After signing up, your Account SID and Auth Token are on the Twilio Console dashboard; buy a phone number under Phone Numbers > Buy a Number.
